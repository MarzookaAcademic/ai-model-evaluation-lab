🧠 Understand the Metrics

🎯 Accuracy

How often is the AI correct overall?

It measures the proportion of all predictions that are correct.

Formula: (TP + TN) / Total

🎯 Precision

When AI predicts positive, how often is it right?

It measures the proportion of predicted positives that are truly positive.

Formula: TP / (TP + FP)

🔍 Recall

Of all actual positives, how many did AI find?

It measures the proportion of actual positive cases correctly detected.

Formula: TP / (TP + FN)

⚖️ F1 Score

How well does the model balance precision and recall?

It is the harmonic mean of precision and recall.

Formula: 2 × Precision × Recall / (Precision + Recall)

💡 Think like an AI evaluator: If False Positives increase while TP stays the same, Precision decreases. Try changing the values above to observe what happens.
