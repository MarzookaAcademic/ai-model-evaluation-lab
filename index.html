🧠 Understand the Metrics

🎯 Accuracy

“How often is the AI correct overall?”

Accuracy measures the proportion of all predictions that the model classified correctly.

🎯 Precision

“When AI says POSITIVE, how often is it right?”

Precision tells us how many of the cases predicted as positive were actually positive.

🔍 Recall

“Of all ACTUAL POSITIVE cases, how many did AI find?”

Recall measures how many of the actual positive cases were correctly detected by the model.

⚖️ F1 Score

“How well does the model balance Precision and Recall?”

F1 Score combines Precision and Recall into a single measure of model performance.
